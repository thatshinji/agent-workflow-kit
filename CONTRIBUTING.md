# Contributing to agent-workflow-kit

Thanks for your interest. This project is a workflow kit, so most contributions are documents, templates, and small gate scripts — all are welcome.

## How to contribute

1. **Open an issue first** for anything beyond a typo fix. Describe the intent, what you want to change, and explicitly what is *out of scope* (non-goals). This mirrors the workflow the kit teaches: intent before implementation.
2. **One issue, one branch, one PR.** Branch names follow `<contributor>/<issue-number>-<short-description>`.
3. **Reference, don't auto-close.** Use `Refs #<issue>` in PR descriptions; issues are closed by the maintainer after verification.
4. **Evidence over opinion.** If you claim a template or script is broken, include a reproduction or a concrete failing example. If you propose a mechanism change, say which real-world failure it prevents.
5. **Keep templates blank.** Templates in `templates/` contain structure, guidance comments, and placeholders only — never content filled in for a specific project.

## Languages

Issues and PRs may be written in English or Chinese. Core documents aim to be bilingual over time; when they diverge, the version linked from the README prevails until the translation is synced.

## Ground rules

- No secrets, tokens, or personal data in issues, PRs, or commits.
- Be direct and kind; critique mechanisms and evidence, not people.
- By contributing, you agree your contributions are licensed under the MIT License.
